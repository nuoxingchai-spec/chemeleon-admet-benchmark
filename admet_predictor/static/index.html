04 · WHY THIS ONE

Built for a fair read.

Most ADMET demos reuse the validation split for early stopping and calibration. This one keeps them separate, so the numbers you see are not tuned on the answer key.

01

Three endpoints

hERG blockade, drug-induced liver injury and Ames mutagenicity, each with its own calibration temperature.

02

Nine checkpoints

Three seeds per endpoint are averaged before calibration, smoothing single-run noise without touching the test labels.

03

Clean calibration

An internal scaffold-disjoint early-stopping split protects the calibration set. Temperatures: 4.25, 0.86 and 1.61.

04

93 released weights

The full benchmark and deployment model family is public, with a SHA-256 manifest and a model card.
